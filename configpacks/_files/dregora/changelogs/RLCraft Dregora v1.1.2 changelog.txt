====================
CHANGELOG for v1.1.2:
====================

Mods added:
- Save My Stronghold (prevents stronghold corruption)

Mods Updated:
- Updated Eaglemixins
- Updated Dregora RL
- Updated FermiumBooter

====================
RogueLike:
====================
- Tweaked Roguelike dungeons to spawn a tad more often

====================
DregoraRL:
====================
- Fixed one serious location bound crash, sorry was in Japan couldn't fix this earlier!
- Redistributed biome layout, fewer wastelands more temperate biomes
- Fixed Deadphar Easter egg Definitely Dead not spawning and tweaked spawn rarity
- Greatly buffed Medusa's temple loot by replacing junk loot chests for dungeon & uncommon loot & replaced all pedestal weapons for better counterparts
- Applied some degree of anti-cheese to medusa's temple for high value of loot in lower chambers

====================
LycanitesMobs:
====================
- Allowed Ithaqua to spawn at night in forests.
- Blacklisted Djinn & Roc spawns for parasite biomes

====================
FermiumMixins:
====================
- If mobs are champions or any other special entity that multiplies XP it will combine both XP rewards

====================
RLMixins:
====================
- Gave Blights the Eagle treatment >:D


====================
Eaglemixins:
====================
- Old worlds are now compatible with this version!
- Living armor adaptations are now kept on evolution.
- Backpacks & Charm Crates now do not shield against radiation anymore. (Use radiation shielding on chests/shulkers to make a container radiation resistant)
- Unopened loot containers that would generate irradiated items will now irradiate their surroundings without being opened by a default average value


====================
FermiumBooter:
====================
- Makes mixin related issues more readable by writing them to separate logs and crash reports
